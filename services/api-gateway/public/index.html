The problem

Kenyans lose money to online fraud every day.

Facebook Marketplace. OLX. WhatsApp groups. Pochi la Biashara. Every day, Kenyans lose money to people they trusted. LipaSafe ends that.

Secondhand goods arrive faulty or stolen.

You buy a phone on Facebook or OLX. It arrives cracked, fake, or never arrives. Seller blocks you. Money gone. LipaSafe holds payment until you confirm the item is exactly what was described.

Bundle seller takes your money via till and vanishes.

You send KES 500 to a bundle seller's till or Pochi. They confirm. Then silence. No bundle, no refund, no recourse. LipaSafe holds the funds — bundle seller only gets paid after you receive and confirm.

Delivery paid. Parcel arrives damaged or wrong.

You pay for a delivery. The rider brings the wrong item, something broken, or nothing at all. No way to get your money back. LipaSafe only releases payment after you confirm the parcel arrived correctly.

Fundi paid upfront. Job abandoned halfway.

Plumber, electrician, painter — you pay upfront. They do half the work or disappear after deposit. LipaSafe holds payment and only releases it in milestones as work is completed to your satisfaction.

House deposit paid. Landlord was fake.

Rental scams are rampant. You pay a deposit for a house that doesn't exist or is already occupied. Fake landlords, fake listings, real money lost. LipaSafe holds your deposit until you physically confirm the house.

Wrong number. Money gone. Fuliza cuts what you receive.

One wrong digit and your M-Pesa is gone to a stranger. SafeSend holds the money until the recipient accepts — wrong number means automatic refund. And when someone sends you money, Fuliza no longer intercepts it before you even see it.

How it works

Simple as M-Pesa.
Safer than cash.

LipaSafe sits between buyer and seller — holding funds securely until both sides are satisfied. No blind trust required.

Step 01

Create a deal

Buyer and seller agree on terms inside the app. LipaSafe generates a secure deal reference for both parties.

Step 02

Pay into escrow

Buyer pays via M-Pesa STK Push. Funds are held securely — not yet released to the seller.

Step 03

Deliver and confirm

Seller delivers. Buyer confirms receipt in the app. Funds are instantly released to seller via M-Pesa.

If disputed

Raise a dispute

If something goes wrong, either party can raise a dispute with evidence. Our AI engine reviews and admins resolve fairly.

Security & Trust

Your money is protected
at every step.

LipaSafe is built with financial-grade safety. Funds never move unless you authorize it — with full audit trails throughout.

Escrow Ledger

Funds are held in a secure internal ledger — never sent to the seller until the buyer explicitly confirms delivery.

AI Dispute Engine

Photo and video evidence is analyzed by a multi-model AI system before human admins review any dispute.

Automatic Retries

If a payout fails, the system retries automatically up to 3 times with admin escalation if unresolved.

Full Audit Trail

Every action — payment, release, dispute, refund — is logged with timestamp and actor for full transparency.

M-Pesa Native

Built directly on Safaricom's Daraja API — STK Push, B2C, and C2B. The same infrastructure behind every Kenyan mobile payment.

OTP Confirmation

Critical actions like fund release require OTP verification. No funds move on accidents or unauthorized requests.

Usage Policy

Who it's for.
What it's not for.

LipaSafe is built for legitimate commerce between Kenyan M-Pesa users. Some uses are restricted to protect all parties on the platform.

Requirements

  • Active Safaricom M-Pesa account
  • Kenyan phone number (+254)
  • Android device running Android 8.0 or higher
  • Both buyer and seller must be registered LipaSafe users
  • Minimum transaction amount: KES 10
  • Deal terms must be agreed upon before payment is initiated
  • Delivery confirmation must happen within the agreed timeframe

Restrictions

  • No illegal goods, controlled substances, or counterfeit products
  • No financial instruments, loans, or pyramid scheme transactions
  • No transactions involving minors
  • Fraudulent dispute claims result in permanent account suspension
  • Funds are not held beyond the agreed deal period without extension
  • Platform fees are non-refundable once a transaction is initiated
  • Currently available in Kenya only
