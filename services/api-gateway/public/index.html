How Lipasafe works

Simple, safe, and built on trust

No more "send money first and pray." Lipasafe holds the money so both the buyer and the seller are protected from start to finish.

Create your deal

Buyer and seller agree on the item, service, or payment. Create the transaction in the Lipasafe app in seconds.

Pay into escrow

The buyer pays via M-Pesa. Money is locked safely in Lipasafe escrow — not sent directly to the seller yet.

Deliver & confirm

The seller delivers the goods or completes the service. The buyer inspects and confirms everything is as agreed.

Funds released

Once confirmed, funds are released instantly to the seller's M-Pesa. If there's a problem, our dispute team steps in.

What we do

One platform. Every payment protected.

Wherever money changes hands between strangers — or across borders — Lipasafe stands in the middle to protect it.

Marketplace Escrow

Delivery Payments
