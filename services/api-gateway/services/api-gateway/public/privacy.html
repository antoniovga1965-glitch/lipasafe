How LipaSafe collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

Last updated: August 2026 · Effective immediately

1. About LipaSafe

LipaSafe is a mobile escrow payment platform operated by Vezoro Enterprice (BN-VZSE95E3), registered in Kenya. We hold funds in escrow via Safaricom M-Pesa Daraja API and release payment only when both parties confirm a deal is complete.

LipaSafe is not a bank and does not hold a deposit-taking license. We are a payment intermediary facilitating escrow arrangements between M-Pesa users.

2. Eligibility

  • Be at least 18 years of age
  • Hold an active Safaricom M-Pesa account on a Kenyan phone number (+254)
  • Own or have authorized access to the Android device you use
  • Agree to these Terms and our Privacy Policy
  • Be located in Kenya

3. How the Escrow Service Works

  • Deal creation: Buyer and seller agree on terms and create a deal reference inside the app
  • Payment into escrow: Buyer pays via M-Pesa STK Push. Funds held in LipaSafe's ledger — not yet sent to seller
  • Delivery: Seller delivers goods or services as agreed
  • Confirmation: Buyer confirms receipt in the app, triggering automatic M-Pesa B2C payout to seller
  • Disputes: Either party may raise a dispute with evidence before confirmation. Admins review and resolve fairly

4. Platform Fees

  • Displayed clearly before a deal is confirmed
  • Deducted automatically from the transaction amount
  • Non-refundable once a transaction has been initiated

LipaSafe does not charge for failed transactions, cancelled deals (before payment), or approved refunds.

5. User Responsibilities

  • Not use LipaSafe for illegal goods, controlled substances, or counterfeit products
  • Not file false or fraudulent disputes
  • Not attempt to manipulate, hack, or exploit the platform
  • Confirm deals honestly — do not refuse confirmation after receiving goods without valid grounds
  • Keep your account credentials and OTP codes confidential

6. Prohibited Transactions

Violations result in immediate account suspension and may be reported to Kenyan authorities.

7. Disputes and Refunds

  • Either party may raise a dispute within the agreed deal timeframe
  • Evidence (photos, videos, messages) must be submitted to support the dispute
  • LipaSafe admins review evidence and make a final determination
  • Approved refunds are processed via M-Pesa within 1–3 business days
  • LipaSafe's admin decision is final for platform disputes

LipaSafe facilitates resolution but cannot guarantee fund recovery in all cases of third-party fraud.
