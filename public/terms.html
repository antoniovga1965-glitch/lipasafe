2. About the service

Lipasafe provides an escrow payment platform. In simple terms: when a buyer and seller (or client and service provider) agree to transact, the buyer pays Lipasafe instead of paying the seller directly. We hold the funds in escrow and release them to the seller only when the agreed conditions — typically delivery or completion of the service — are confirmed.

The Services support transactions including, without limitation: marketplace purchases, delivery-on-payment, rent and house payments, fundi (service provider) jobs, second-hand goods, money requests, and diaspora payments.

Important: Lipasafe is an escrow and payments facilitator, not a bank. Funds held in escrow are not deposits and do not earn interest. Escrow balances are not covered by deposit insurance.

5. How escrow works

5.1 Creating a transaction

  1. The parties agree on the item, service, price, and terms.
  2. A transaction is created in the app and both parties are bound by its stated terms.
  3. The buyer funds the transaction via the supported payment method (e.g., M-Pesa).

5.2 Holding of funds

Funded amounts are held in escrow and are not accessible to the seller until release conditions are met, nor to the buyer once funded (except through the dispute or refund process in these Terms).

5.3 Release

Funds are released to the seller when the buyer confirms satisfactory receipt/completion, or when the confirmation window lapses without a dispute being opened, as specified in the transaction.

5.4 Cancellation

An unfunded transaction may be cancelled freely by either party. A funded transaction may be cancelled only by mutual agreement or through the dispute process.

8. Disputes

If a transaction goes wrong, either party may open a dispute from within the app before funds are released. When a dispute is opened:

  1. Funds remain locked in escrow pending resolution.
  2. Both parties submit evidence (messages, photos, receipts, delivery proof) through the app.
  3. Our dispute resolution team reviews the evidence and may request more information.
  4. We issue a decision: full release to the seller, full refund to the buyer, or a split, based on the transaction terms and evidence.

Our decision is binding on the use of the escrowed funds, without prejudice to either party's right to pursue the matter in a court of competent jurisdiction. We may involve law enforcement where fraud or crime is suspected.
